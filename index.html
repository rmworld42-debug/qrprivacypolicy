Privacy Policy

ScanMaster: QR & Barcode Scanner • Effective Date: November 3, 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: ScanMaster is engineered on an offline-first, on-device architecture. We do not track, collect, transmit, sell, or monetize your scanned data or camera images. All barcode optical recognition is computed directly on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ScanMaster: QR & Barcode Scanner ("we", "our", or "the App") respects and protects your privacy. We are committed to transparency regarding any technical permissions and device functions utilized by the App.

2. Data Processing & On-Device Analysis

All camera frames processed during scanning are handled strictly in device volatile memory (RAM) using Google ML Kit on-device barcode scanning:

  • No Cloud Uploads: Camera frames are analyzed frame-by-frame on your processor and are immediately discarded. No video, photo, or visual information is ever uploaded to any cloud server or external backend.
  • Offline Capability: Core scanning and QR code generation operate 100% offline without requiring internet access.

3. Information Storage on Your Device

ScanMaster provides scan history and saved code functionality for user convenience:

  • Local Database: Scanned barcodes, generated QR codes, and favorite records are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database (Room).
  • User Control: You have complete control to search, delete individual scan entries, or wipe the entire history at any time through the in-app Settings.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application automatically removes all locally saved databases and records.

4. Device Permissions We Request

ScanMaster asks only for permissions strictly necessary for user-initiated functions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Required solely to capture optical video frames for real-time QR and barcode detection. Requested at runtime only when you navigate to the scanner screen.
  • Haptic / Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used to provide tactile feedback pulse when a barcode is successfully detected. This can be toggled on/off in Settings.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used strictly for user-requested optional actions, such as fetching public product metadata from Open Food Facts or opening web links in your browser upon confirmation.
  • Gallery Access (Photo Picker): Uses the standard zero-permission Android Photo Picker when you choose to scan a barcode from an existing photo in your gallery.

5. Safe Link Verification

ScanMaster includes proactive security features to safeguard users against malicious links. When a QR code contains an external URL, the app alerts the user, highlights encryption status (HTTPS vs HTTP), and prompts for explicit confirmation before launching your default web browser.

6. Third-Party Services & Libraries

The application integrates the following industry-standard, trusted libraries:

  • Google ML Kit (Barcode Scanning): Runs locally on-device under Google's standard developer terms.
  • Open Food Facts API: When scanning commercial product barcodes (UPC/EAN), the app queries the public Open Food Facts open-source database to display product details if available. No personal identifiers are transmitted with these queries.
  • ZXing Core: Used solely for mathematical generation and matrix encoding of QR code graphics on-device.

7. Children's Privacy

ScanMaster does not address or knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose utility suitable for all ages without age-gated profiling or data collection.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory standards. Any changes will be published here with an updated "Effective Date".
